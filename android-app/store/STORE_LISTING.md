Full description (4000):
Bottle Tree is a point-of-sale and inventory app for antique dealers, resellers, and garage-sale sellers, with an AI appraiser built in.

WHAT'S FREE, FOREVER
• Sales, items, photos, and sellers
• Cash and card checkout
• Your own storefront page to share with buyers

AI ESTIMATES (the part we charge for)
Photograph a piece from a few angles, type what you know and any marks you can read, and tap Identify & price. NVIDIA Nemotron identifies the piece, returns a price range with a suggested retail, and drafts a listing you can approve and push to your storefront. Your notes outrank the model's guess, on purpose: you know the piece, it knows the market.

PRICING
• 1 free estimate when you sign up
• Single estimate: $0.99
• 10 estimates: $4.99 (never expire)
• Pro: $9.99/month, up to 300 estimates
• Unlimited: $29.99/month
Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. The same account works on the web at bottletree-app.dj-b02.workers.dev and on your phone.

WHO IT'S FOR
Antique shops, booth dealers, estate-sale companies, flippers, and anyone who has ever set a piece on a counter and asked "what's this worth?"

Estimates are opinions generated by an AI model from your photos and notes; they are not formal appraisals. Always verify high-value items with a qualified appraiser.
