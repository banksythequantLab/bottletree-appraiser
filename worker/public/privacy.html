Bottle Tree — Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026 · Banksy AI LLC

Bottle Tree is a point-of-sale, inventory and AI price-estimate app for antique dealers and resellers, offered by Banksy AI LLC ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

Account: your email address and a hashed password. Inventory: the sales, items, prices, notes, seller names and photos you add. Estimates: the descriptions and markings you type and the results our AI returns. Purchases: if you buy estimates or a subscription in the Android app, Google Play and RevenueCat send us the product purchased, an anonymous purchase identifier and the subscription status — never your card details. Technical: standard server logs (IP address, browser, timestamps) kept briefly for security.

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers, and we show no ads.

How photos and descriptions are used

When you request an estimate, the photos and text for that item are sent to AI models to identify and price the piece. Those models run on Nebius AI Cloud (NVIDIA Nemotron and Gemma models) and comparable-sales lookups use Tavily web search. These providers process the data to return a result and are not permitted to use it to train models. Photos are stored in Cloudflare R2 so they can appear in your inventory and, only if you choose to list an item, on your public storefront page.

Where data lives

Cloudflare (Workers, D1, R2) hosts the app and database. RevenueCat manages in-app purchases. Google Play processes payments for the Android app. Each acts as a processor under its own privacy terms.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use your photos for anything other than the estimate and inventory features you use.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any item, photo or sale in the app at any time. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Google Play.

Delete your Bottle Tree account

To delete your Bottle Tree account and all data associated with it:

  1. Email dj@soltis.info with the subject "Delete my Bottle Tree account", sent from the email address on the account.
  2. We confirm the request by reply and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted: your account and login, all sales, items, photos, sellers, storefront pages, appraisal history and estimate credits. What is kept: Google Play purchase records (held by Google) and anonymised usage counts required for tax and accounting, for the period the law requires. Unused credits and active subscription time are not refunded by us; cancel subscriptions in Google Play before requesting deletion.

Children

Bottle Tree is for adults running a business and is not directed at children under 13.

Estimates are not appraisals

Price estimates are generated by AI to help you decide on a listing price. They are not certified appraisals and should not be relied on for insurance, estate, tax or legal purposes.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes here with a new effective date. Questions: dj@soltis.info.
